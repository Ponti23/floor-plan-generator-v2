# PlanLab Product Specification

## Purpose

PlanLab is a desktop-first browser tool that converts a structured architectural brief into three reproducible schematic floor-plan alternatives. It is an option-exploration aid, not a drafting, BIM, code-compliance, or construction-document system.

The product succeeds when an architect can quickly test whether a simple room program can plausibly fit a rectangular site envelope, compare genuinely different planning strategies, and understand each option's trade-offs.

## Primary user and job

The initial user is an architect or building designer in early concept design. Their job is: "Given this site envelope and room program, show me several plausible arrangements worth developing, and tell me what each arrangement trades away."

## MVP workflow

1. Enter rectangular site dimensions and four architect-defined offsets.
2. Enter a room program, quantities, minimum dimensions, and a small set of relationships.
3. Enter an optional target or maximum gross floor area. If omitted, PlanLab derives a clearly disclosed search range from room minima and circulation allowance.
4. Generate with an explicit seed.
5. Review three valid and materially different options: Compact Efficiency, Best Flow, and Balanced.
6. Inspect hard-rule results, score components, warnings, and key observations.
7. Restore the project after refresh from local browser storage.

## Outputs

Each option contains:

- site, buildable envelope, footprint, spaces, abstract access portals, and entrance;
- PASS/FAIL hard validation with structured evidence;
- category scores and raw metrics with disclosed formulas;
- positive observations, trade-offs, and warnings generated by rule evaluators;
- the project fingerprint, solver version, scoring version, seed, and generation budget needed for reproduction.

Only valid candidates may be shown as recommendations. If fewer than three are found, the UI reports the shortfall and the blocking constraints; it must not silently weaken hard rules.

## Product principles

- Geometry is produced by a deterministic solver, never directly by an LLM.
- Hard validity is separate from desirability scoring.
- Scores compare PlanLab candidates for one brief; they are not claims of architectural quality or compliance.
- Measurements and rule results remain inspectable and are not hidden behind a single score.
- The canvas dominates the workspace; configuration and analysis support it.
- Local-first data and no login are deliberate MVP constraints.

## Non-goals

PlanLab does not verify regulations, structure, egress codes, daylight, services, constructability, furniture fit, door swings, or accessibility compliance. It does not replace professional judgment.

Required product notice:

> Conceptual layout only — verify dimensions, regulations, site conditions, access and construction requirements before use.

## Canonical prototype

The 20 m × 30 m site, four offsets, and room program in [`../PLANLAB_MASTER_PLAN.md`](../PLANLAB_MASTER_PLAN.md) is the first fixture. Its envelope is 17 m × 22 m = 374 m². The fixture must add one missing input before it is a meaningful optimization problem: a target/maximum gross floor area or an approved derived-area policy.

## Success measures

- At least three hard-valid, visibly different options for the canonical fixture under an approved area policy.
- Same normalized brief, seed, solver version, and budget produce byte-equivalent geometry and ranking.
- Every displayed metric and observation traces to evaluator output.
- A user can identify the major trade-off among the three options without reading implementation details.

## Failure behaviour

- Invalid input is rejected before generation with field-level explanations.
- Infeasible briefs return an infeasibility summary, not an empty canvas.
- Budget exhaustion returns the valid options found plus search diagnostics; fewer than three is an honest result.
- Corrupt or incompatible local state is quarantined, exportable for support, and resettable.
