# Bucket 6.4 — reset flow and destructive confirmation

**Status:** complete, with the confirmation wording **pending user approval** (copy hard gate).
**Executor:** orchestrator (`/root`). **Date:** 2026-09-15.

## What shipped

- `GenerationController.reset()` — cancels anything in flight, clears `lastAttempt` and drops
  `lastCompatibleResult`, so a reset can never leave a previous brief's layouts on screen looking
  current. Unit-tested.
- A **Reset project** action in the brief footer (a destructive action is styled distinctly, not
  hidden among the neutral text actions).
- A two-step confirmation: clicking the action renders an inline `role="alertdialog"` panel stating
  that the locally saved project is deleted and that the action cannot be undone; only its **Reset**
  button performs the deletion. **Keep project** dismisses it.
- `performReset()` clears only the store's own keys (`projectStore.clear()` is prefix-scoped),
  rebuilds the editor from `CANONICAL_PROJECT`, clears the committed-project map, the selected layout,
  the generation revision, the focused evidence and the viewport, resets the save bookkeeping, and
  re-renders.

## Copy status — hard gate, not frozen

New user-facing strings were needed and are **not** covered by the approved 5.0 package:

> **Reset project?**
> This deletes the project saved in this browser and starts again from the default brief. It cannot
> be undone.
> [Reset] [Keep project]

They ship in `src/app/presentation-copy.ts` under `reset` + `actions.resetConfirm/resetCancel`, which
keeps them replaceable and easy to put in front of the user. They are recorded on the board as
`needs-human` copy rather than as approved wording.
